Description

John Coyne Estates are delighted to present to the market this stunning 3 bed semi-detached home which has been extended at the rear and also includes a Detached Muti-Purpose Outbuilding. Ard na Carraige is a highly sought after residential location in the town centre and ideally located within a short walk to all amenities. This beautiful home comes to the market in pristine condition throughout with features to include; new Grant oil boiler with Combi technology, extension at the rear, detached multi-purpose outbuilding at the rear which has been constructed to the highest standards, water softening system, new flooring installed throughout, new kitchen, maintenance free back garden, new blinds throughout, etc.

Accommodation

Accommodation briefly comprises of a bright welcoming hallway with attractive flooring and guest w/c under staircase, to the left is the sitting room which features an abundance of natural lighting and double doors to the living room. The living – dining – kitchen area are open plan and provide a modern living experience to this beautiful home. The kitchen-dining area is an extension completed in very recent years and is built to the highest standards. Upstairs there are 3 generously sized bedrooms, all with new flooring and the master bed has it’s own en-suite. There is a Stira stairs to the attic, which provides additional storage. To the rear of this stunning home is a Detached Multi-Purpose Outbuilding, which has been built to the highest standards and needs just a little internal finishing. This building provides a great added dimension to this property and offers a wide range of potential uses from a home office/gym to residential uses, subject to expected upcoming changes in policy.
